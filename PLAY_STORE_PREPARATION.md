# BookFood Play Store preparation

## Store listing inputs still required

- Final app name and developer/company legal name
- Support email, website, and privacy-policy public URL
- Short description (maximum 80 characters)
- Full product description
- App icon, feature graphic, and phone screenshots
- Target countries, category, pricing, and contact details

## Policy and Data safety inputs

Confirm the final production behavior before answering the Play Console form:

- Account/profile data collected from canteen owners
- Employee name and mobile number collected during ordering
- Order, payment-status, and Pay Later records stored by the service
- Device identifiers/FCM tokens used for push notifications
- Whether analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are enabled in the final build
- Data encryption in transit, deletion process, retention period, and support contact

The privacy policy must describe these actual practices, the organization responsible for the data, user rights, retention/deletion, security, and contact information. Do not publish a generic policy with unverified claims.

## Required visual assets

- 512 × 512 PNG app icon
- 1024 × 500 feature graphic
- At least two accurate phone screenshots from the signed release build
- Optional tablet screenshots only if the tablet experience is supported and tested

## Final submission gate

1. Complete the client acceptance checklist.
2. Upload the signed AAB to internal testing first.
3. Resolve Play Console policy, target API, signing, or pre-launch report issues.
4. Promote the same approved build to the intended release track.
